FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the tool, the roadmap, the dashboard and the data pipeline.

What is ADHMAT?

The Africa Digital Health Maturity Assessment Tool, developed by Africa CDC, helps Member States assess the maturity of their digital health systems at national and sub-national level, and the readiness of individual facilities.

Who completes the assessment?

A Ministry-led assessment team, coordinated by a designated national focal point. Directors of Digital Health, Primary Health Care and RMNCH are invited to nominate the focal point.

What do Member States need to deliver?

The national ADHMAT assessment, the qualitative inputs, and a country case study.

How many questions are there?

373 in the National tool and 327 in the Sub-National tool (version 2.5). Digital Payment Systems is a supplementary module.

What are the maturity levels?

L1 Nascent (below 40%), L2 Emerging (40–54%), L3 Established (55–69%), L4 Advanced (70–84%) and L5 Optimized (85% and above).

Is the overall score used to rank countries?

No. The domain profile is the headline result. The overall score is a secondary summary and is not intended for ranking.

Do I need to record evidence?

Yes. For each Yes answer, record the evidence source, date and verification status.

What are the key dates?

Webinar 1 on 28 October 2026; 13 November 2026 for Member States from the Kigali convening to complete their assessment; the continental workshop on 19–20 November 2026; the review window for the first draft opens on 22 December 2026; and the Report launches at the African Union Summit in February 2027. See the roadmap.

Is the dashboard showing real results?

The badge at the top of the page and the banner on the dashboard say which data is shown. Live is data read from the ADHMAT project in REDCap. Test data is generated for all 55 Member States to check the design and is not a real result. The dashboard shows live data as soon as some exists and falls back to test data until then.

How does data get from REDCap to the dashboard?

A pipeline on the server calls the REDCap API on a schedule (and when REDCap’s Data Entry Trigger fires), recalculates every score from the answers, and publishes a new snapshot. Open dashboards refresh by themselves. Nothing has to be mapped by hand: the pipeline reads the REDCap data dictionary to find the questions, domains, sub-domains, countries and priority items.

Where is the data stored, and who can change it?

On the computer that runs this site, in the data folder. Anyone who can open the site can view the dashboard. Only someone with the admin key can run a sync, upload a file or change settings. Personal contact details of focal points and the free-text answers in Module 2 are never copied from REDCap.

What if the REDCap form changes?

At each sync the pipeline compares the REDCap form with the dictionary it has loaded. If questions or choices changed, it rebuilds its mapping from the new form and records the change in the sync history. If the new form cannot be read as an ADHMAT form, the last good data stays on the dashboard and the problem is shown on the Data & pipeline page.

Where can I get the assessment workbooks?

Africa CDC shares them with national focal points. They are not hosted on this site.

Is this the official Africa CDC website?

No. This is a local deployment prepared for review and testing.
